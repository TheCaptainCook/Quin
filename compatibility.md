## 🛠️ Status Definitions

- **Perfect**: Flawless gameplay, full framerate, audio/graphics fully in sync.
- **Playable**: Game can be completed with playable performance and minor bugs.
- **In-game**: Reaches 3D/2D gameplay, but crashes or major visual/audio glitches prevent progress.
- **Menu**: Reaches title screen or main menu, but fails to transition into gameplay.
- **Boots**: Displays intro splash screen or early debug logs before freezing/crashing.
- **Nothing**: Crashes on boot before rendering any output.
